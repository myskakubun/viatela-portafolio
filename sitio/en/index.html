Raizeta: memory that does not fade

My research line. A hexagonal stone that holds the memory of a diaspora family, and an AI, RAIzeta, that reads it without making anything up.

Learn about Raizeta

The stone is read in layers, just like the one at the top of this page:

  1. With the naked eye: the family name, the place of origin and a QR code.
  2. With a magnifier: the family members' names in microtext.
  3. Inside the glass: children, grandparents and great-grandparents engraved on planes at different depths.
  4. With a reader: data in a dense code that RAIzeta queries and cites, with an evidence level for each source.

In design and pilot stage: mock-up, engraving tests and reader prototype.
